# Reasoning and Intellectual Foundations of Musk's "Money Becomes Irrelevant, Energy Is the Real Currency" View

Research note for a Japanese report explaining why Musk believes this. Date of research: 2026-10-21.
Sourcing caveat for the whole note: direct page fetches (Wikipedia, Forbes, etc.) were blocked in this research session, so findings come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages. No full official transcript of the Nikhil Kamath podcast was found; quotes from it come from consistent secondary reports and differ slightly in wording. **[MUSK]** = Musk's own words or stated reasoning. **[ANALYST]** = interpretation by commentators or the researcher.

## Q1. Musk's own logic: AI + humanoid robots → near-zero-cost goods and services → "universal high income" → money fades → only physics limits (energy, mass) remain

### Takeaway
Between Nov 2025 and 2026, Musk has stated a consistent chain. (1) Money is basically a "database for labor allocation". (2) AI plus robots (Optimus) make labor effectively unlimited, so goods and services become abundant. (3) Money's role therefore "declines dramatically" or "disappears as a concept", and he calls the outcome "universal high income". (4) What remains scarce are "fundamental physics elements" such as electricity/energy and mass. Hence "energy is the true currency."

### Cited Findings
**Core statements, in chronological order**
- **[MUSK] US-Saudi Investment Forum, Kennedy Center, Washington DC, 19 Nov 2025, on stage with Nvidia's Jensen Huang:** "The fundamental physics elements will still be constraints, but I think at some point currency becomes irrelevant." He named electricity and mass as the remaining constraints, predicted work becomes "optional", and spoke of "universal high income" rather than basic income. — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/musk-predicts-money-stop-being-relevant-future-ai-robotics-progress); [Transcript, Singju Post](https://singjupost.com/transcript-elon-musk-and-jensen-huang-talk-ai-at-u-s-saudi-investment-forum-11-19-25/); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/elon-musk-claims-money-wont-exist-in-the-future-and-jensen-huang-would-like-a-heads-up-2000688307); [Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/elon-musk-predicts-money-could-become-irrelevant-at-u-s-saudi-investment-forum/)

**Critiques [ANALYST]**
- Critics ask who pays for "universal high income" and how it would be distributed. They note his forecast is tied to his own products (Optimus, xAI, Tesla Energy), which makes it as much an industrial plan as a prediction. One critic said an income that depends on infrastructure people don't own "isn't a right, it's a concession". — [TechNewsWorld](https://www.technewsworld.com/story/the-trillionaires-paradox-why-elon-musk-wants-to-kill-money-180138.html?cat_id=42); [BMX newsletter](https://bmx-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/elon-musk-s-20-year-warning-why-ai-makes-work-optional-money-disappears-and-we-live-in-a-simulation)
- Economist Steve Keen's channel and The American Spectator criticized the "moneyless" vision as ignoring economics. — [Debunking Economics](https://debunkingeconomics.com/video/musks-moneyless-mirage); [American Spectator](https://spectator.org/?p=699430)

### Inferences
- **[ANALYST]** Musk's chain can be reconstructed as a syllogism:
  1. Money is a ledger for allocating scarce labor and goods.
  2. AI plus robots make labor (and therefore most goods and services) non-scarce.
  3. A ledger for something non-scarce has little purpose, so money "fades".
  4. Physics constraints (energy, raw mass, and compute, which is itself energy-limited) do not vanish.
  5. Therefore the residual unit of account is physical: energy, or "wattage".
- **[ANALYST]** "Money disappears" and "energy is the true currency" are two halves of one claim, not a contradiction. Fiat money as a labor-allocation tool becomes unnecessary, while energy remains the scarce input whose quantity defines what is possible.

### Gaps
- No verbatim official transcript of the Kamath podcast was obtained. The exact wording of "energy is the true currency" comes from multiple secondary reports.
- The venue and date of the "Optimus… infinite money glitch… money measured in wattage" remark could not be pinned down. It may be a Tesla event or the 2025 shareholder meeting; this is unconfirmed.
- Reports of a 2026 Abundance Summit remark ("We'll basically just issue money to people") appeared in search summaries, but no specific source page could be verified, so it is excluded from the findings.

## Q2. Intellectual roots: Kardashev, Iain M. Banks, Technocracy energy certificates (and the Haldeman link), Soddy, Fuller, Bitcoin proof-of-work

### Takeaway
Musk explicitly cites two of these roots: the Kardashev scale (often) and Iain M. Banks's Culture (often, since at least 2014). The others (Technocracy Inc.'s energy certificates, his grandfather Joshua Haldeman's Technocracy leadership, Soddy, Fuller) are parallels drawn by analysts. No source found shows Musk himself citing Technocracy, Soddy or Fuller. The Bitcoin "energy money" argument is one Musk has explicitly endorsed (Oct and Dec 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Iain M. Banks's Culture (explicitly cited by Musk)**
- **[MUSK]** 2018 tweet: "I am a utopian anarchist of the kind described by Iain Banks." In 2014 he called the series a compelling picture of a semi-utopian galactic future, while hoping it was not too optimistic about AI. — [Freethinker, Oct 2023](https://freethinker.co.uk/2023/10/science-fiction-and-the-tech-billionaires/); [Bookman Substack](https://bookman.substack.com/p/elon-musk-understands-the-culture)
- SpaceX's drone ships "Just Read the Instructions" and "Of Course I Still Love You" are named after Culture ships. "Neural lace", the original name concept behind Neuralink, is also a Banks idea. — [Freethinker](https://freethinker.co.uk/2023/10/science-fiction-and-the-tech-billionaires/); [Metavert: Iain Banks](https://metavert.io/iain-banks)
- **[MUSK]** Nov 2025: he cited the Culture as a model of a "probable positive AI future" where currency is irrelevant but electricity and mass remain constraints (see Q1). — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/trends/story/elon-musk-says-robots-will-kill-jobs-and-cash-are-you-ready-for-whats-coming-next-502862-2025-11-20)
- **[ANALYST]** Critics argue the Culture's post-scarcity depends on benevolent superintelligent "Minds" running a moneyless, quasi-socialist society. This sits in tension with Musk's market-capitalist role. — [Drip Feed Utopia](https://dripfeedutopia.substack.com/p/the-culture-doesnt-save-us); [Freethinker](https://freethinker.co.uk/2023/10/science-fiction-and-the-tech-billionaires/)

**Kardashev scale (explicitly cited by Musk)**
- Nikolai Kardashev's 1964 scale ranks civilizations by energy harnessed: Type I uses a planet's energy, Type II a star's, Type III a galaxy's. — [KEYT](https://keyt.com/?p=1442834)
- A 2023 study put humanity at roughly Type 0.7276. Its lead author said reaching Type I on current trends would likely take millennia. — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/elon-musks-plans-refer-to-an-idea-conceived-in-the-1960s-what-is-the-kardashev-scale)
- Musk's specific Kardashev statements are covered in Q4.

**Technocracy movement and energy certificates (analyst-drawn parallel, with a family link)**
- Technocracy Inc. (Howard Scott, 1930s) proposed replacing price-based money with "energy accounting". Citizens would receive "energy certificates" equal to their share of the energy available for production in a period, and unused certificates would expire, so they could not be hoarded as capital. — [Brilliant Maps](https://brilliantmaps.com/the-technate-of-america/); [History on the Net, 1933 Technocrat magazine](https://www.historyonthenet.com/authentichistory/1930-1939/2-fdr/2-reception/technocrats/index.html); [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/a-1930s-movement-wanted-to-merge-the-us-canada-and-greenland-heres-why-it-has-modern-resonances-252587)
- Scott said energy accounting came largely from Willard Gibbs's thermodynamics. He also said the group reached its ideas before reading Soddy's *Wealth, Virtual Wealth and Debt* in 1927, though historians note close similarities. — [Encyclopedia of Human Thermodynamics: Howard Scott](https://www.eoht.info/page/Howard%20Scott)
- The movement declined because of Scott's temperament, the lack of a political plan, and revelations that he had inflated his credentials. — [Brilliant Maps](https://brilliantmaps.com/the-technate-of-america/)
- **Haldeman link:** Joshua N. Haldeman, Musk's maternal grandfather, was a Regina (Saskatchewan) chiropractor and a leader ("director") of Technocracy Inc. in Canada in the 1930s. He was arrested by the RCMP on 8 Oct 1940 under the wartime Defence of Canada Regulations for membership in an illegal organization (Technocracy was banned in Canada at the time). He moved to South Africa in 1950 and died in a plane crash in 1974. — [Toronto Life](https://torontolife.com/deep-dives/doctor-strange-the-curious-life-of-elon-musks-canadian-grandfather/); [CBC via Yahoo News Canada](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/ideological-adventures-elon-musks-saskatchewan-100039343.html); [Canada's History, "The Last Utopians"](https://www.canadashistory.ca/explore/politics-law/the-last-utopians)
  - Sources conflict on the outcome of his trial (found guilty vs. acquitted).
  - Beware misinformation: a viral photo said to show Haldeman in a Technocracy office actually shows Howard Scott. — [Lead Stories fact check, Jan 2025](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/01/fact-check-photo-does-not-show-elon-musks-grandfather-joshua-haldeman-at-technocracy-office.html)
- **[ANALYST]** The Conversation (2025) noted modern echoes: Technocracy's proposed "Technate" covered the US, Canada and Greenland, at a time when US annexation talk was in the news. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/a-1930s-movement-wanted-to-merge-the-us-canada-and-greenland-heres-why-it-has-modern-resonances-252587)

**Frederick Soddy, energy theory of value**
- Nobel chemist Frederick Soddy's *Wealth, Virtual Wealth and Debt* (1926) argued that real wealth is physical and subject to entropy, while debt (money) grows by compound interest without limit. That mismatch, he argued, drives financial crises. His work was ignored in his time but later seen as a forerunner of ecological economics. Soddy called his energy-based thinking "ergosophy". — [Wikipedia: Wealth, Virtual Wealth and Debt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wealth,_Virtual_Wealth_and_Debt); [Wikipedia: Ergosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergosophy)

**Buckminster Fuller**
- Fuller's *Critical Path* (1981) and his "World Game" proposed a worldwide electric grid. He reportedly said the new economic basis would be kilowatt-hours rather than gold or dollars. He also urged humanity to live within its "cosmic energy income" (sun, wind, water) instead of drawing down fossil "savings". — [Wikipedia: Critical Path](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Path_(book)); [Civilization Emerging: Critical Path](https://civilizationemerging.com/resources/critical-path/); [World Game text archive](https://nsdl.arcabc.ca/_flysystem/repo-service/2025-06/96833-Extracted%20Text.txt)
  - The kilowatt-hour currency quote appears only in secondary or republished sources found here.

**Bitcoin proof-of-work as "energy money"**
- **[MUSK]** 14 Oct 2025 (reported date): replying on X to ZeroHedge, which argued that AI spending would be funded by currency debasement ("you can't print energy"), Musk wrote: "you can issue fake fiat currency, and every government in history has done so, but it is impossible to fake energy." He said Bitcoin is based on energy. Cointelegraph called it his first serious Bitcoin post since Nov 2022. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/elon-musk-bitcoin-fiat-ai-arms-race); [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/crypto/cryptocurrency/25/10/48192005/elon-musk-says-bitcoin-has-energy-you-can-issue-fake-fiat-but-it-is-impossible-to-fake-ener); [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/business/elon-musk-touts-bitcoin-and-inflation)
- **Contrast with 2021:** In May 2021 Musk halted Tesla's Bitcoin payments, citing fossil-fuel-heavy mining. In April 2021 he had replied "True" to Jack Dorsey's tweet that Bitcoin "incentivizes renewable energy". — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/elon-musk-calls-bitcoin-energy-220238609.html); [Slashdot summarizing BBC, Apr 2021](https://news.slashdot.org/story/21/04/24/0456207/elon-musk-jack-dorsey-argue-that-bitcoin-incentivises-renewable-energy)
- **Dorsey:** His tweet relied on a Square/ARK Invest white paper (Apr 2021). It argued Bitcoin miners are flexible "unique energy buyers" that can make solar and wind projects more profitable. Square pledged $10M to renewable-powered mining, and Dorsey said crypto would "ultimately be fully powered by clean energy". Critics called it "greenwashing". — [Slashdot/BBC](https://news.slashdot.org/story/21/04/24/0456207/elon-musk-jack-dorsey-argue-that-bitcoin-incentivises-renewable-energy); [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/square-to-allocate-10m-for-renewable-energy-bitcoin-mining/); [Bitcoinist](https://bitcoinist.com/what-did-musk-dorsey-and-wood-say-about-esg-green-energy-and-bitcoin-mining/amp/)
- **Saylor:** He replied to Musk's 2025 energy-currency remark: "The laws of nature are superior to the laws of man." He has long defended proof-of-work against environmental criticism. In 2021 Musk and Saylor jointly convened the "Bitcoin Mining Council", which drew criticism. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/elon-musk-calls-bitcoin-energy-220238609.html); [BTC-Echo](https://www.btc-echo.de/news/zum-merge-michael-saylor-verteidigt-bitcoin-und-proof-of-work-150809/); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/musk-and-saylor-catch-flak-for-bitcoin-mining-council-gambit/)
- **Skeptic:** Peter Schiff disputed that energy use gives Bitcoin value, calling it a "fake asset". — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/elon-musk-calls-bitcoin-energy-220238609.html)

### Inferences
- **[ANALYST]** There is a striking structural resemblance between Musk's "energy is the true currency" and his grandfather's Technocracy energy-certificate scheme. Both hold that physical energy, not finance, is the real basis of value, and both feature a post-market abundance run by engineering. This makes a compelling narrative hook for a Japanese report. However, it should be framed as a parallel and a possible family influence, not a documented one. No source found has Musk citing Technocracy or crediting his grandfather's economic ideas.
- **[ANALYST]** Banks supplies the social picture: a post-scarcity, moneyless society with AI caretakers. Kardashev supplies the metric: progress equals energy harnessed. Bitcoin supplies a present-day financial instrument for the claim that "energy can't be faked". Soddy and Fuller are older intellectual lineage that commentators can invoke.
- **[ANALYST]** Musk's stance on Bitcoin's energy use flipped from criticism (2021) to praise (2025). This matches a shift from framing energy as an environmental cost to framing it as the guarantor of value.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found showing Musk publicly commenting on his grandfather's Technocracy beliefs or on energy certificates.
- Whether Walter Isaacson's biography *Elon Musk* (2023) discusses Haldeman's Technocracy role was not confirmed in this research.
- The exact title Haldeman held (e.g., "national chairman" or "research director" of Technocracy Inc. Canada) could not be verified; sources say "director"/leader.
- No primary Fuller text confirming the "kilowatt-hour as currency" wording was obtained.
- No statement was found from Dorsey (2023 to 2026) directly reacting to Musk's 2025 "energy is the true currency" remark.

## Q3. "Compute = energy": AI scaling limited by electricity; Altman, Huang and Musk compared

### Takeaway
All three major AI figures converge on the idea that the binding constraint on AI, and therefore on the value it creates, is electricity.
- Altman: "the cost of intelligence should eventually converge to near the cost of electricity" (2025), and "compute is going to be the currency of the future" (2024).
- Huang: in a power-limited AI factory, "performance per watt is your revenues" (tokens per watt).
- Musk: AI would run out of transformers and then electricity (2024), and energy is the true currency (2025).
- The difference: Altman names compute as the currency and Huang frames tokens as the product, while Musk goes one layer deeper to energy itself.

### Cited Findings
- **[ALTMAN]** *The Gentle Singularity* (blog, 10 June 2025): as datacenter production gets automated, "the cost of intelligence should eventually converge to near the cost of electricity." He said an average ChatGPT query uses about 0.34 Wh. — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/sam-altman-reveals-chatgpt-energy-bill-and-road-to-superintelligence/amp/); [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/sam-altman-says-the-singularity-is-here-and-its-disappointingly-boring/); [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250611-sam-altman-chatgpt-water/); original at blog.samaltman.com/the-gentle-singularity
  - **[ALTMAN]** He had earlier said the cost of using AI falls about 10x every 12 months. — [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/sam-altman-says-the-singularity-is-here-and-its-disappointingly-boring/)
- **[ALTMAN]** Lex Fridman Podcast (March 2024): "I think compute is going to be the currency of the future. I think it will be maybe the most precious commodity in the world." Benzinga framed this as compute, "not fiat or Bitcoin", as the future currency. — [Benzinga, Mar 2024](https://benzinga.com/news/24/03/37841813/compute-not-fiat-or-bitcoin-will-be-the-currency-of-the-future-says-sam-altman-as-nvidias-jensen-hua); [HackerNoon](https://sia.hackernoon.com/compute-is-going-to-the-currency-of-the-future-says-sam-altman-on-the-lex-fridman-podcast); [Fireflies transcript](https://fireflies.ai/blog/lex-fridman-sam-altman-openai-gpt5-agi-sora/)
- **[HUANG]** GTC 2026 keynote, as reported: "If your factory has one gigawatt, it will not have more." "Performance per watt is your revenues." Commentators describe "tokens per watt" as the key economic metric of AI factories. — [36Kr EU](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3726315458048516); [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/nvidia/nvidia-ceo-highlights-the-rise-of-ai-factories-tokens/); [AI2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/jensen-huang-frames-ai-tokens-as-the-new-enterprise-currency-at-gtc)

- **[MUSK]** Bosch Connected World (2024): AI compute was growing about 10x every six months, which he said can't last. "The next shortage will be voltage step-down transformers… As we solve the transformer shortage, there will be the fundamental electricity generation shortage… people are going to start hitting challenges with power generation maybe by the middle of next year, end of next year." He cited simultaneous demand from EVs and AI. — [NBC Chicago/CNBC](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/business/money-report/elon-musk-says-ai-could-run-into-power-capacity-issues-by-middle-of-next-year/3750482/); [Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/software-apps/elon-musk-the-verge-of-ai-revolution-no-power); [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/technology/elon-musk-ai)
- **[MUSK]** At the Nov 2025 forum with Huang, he discussed "AI factories" and said electricity and mass remain constraints even when currency is irrelevant (see Q1). — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/musk-predicts-money-stop-being-relevant-future-ai-robotics-progress)
- **[MUSK]** Space-based compute: in a June 2026 interview he said you need solar-powered AI satellites in deep space to use even a millionth of the Sun's energy. Coverage of SpaceX's FCC filing for up to one million solar-powered orbital data-center satellites calls it "a first step toward becoming a Kardashev Type II civilization". — [Singju Post transcript, 8 June 2026](https://singjupost.com/interview-elon-musk-on-spacex-plans-transcript/); [KEYT](https://keyt.com/?p=1442834); [36Kr EU, "Space AI" plan](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3567865887390593)
  - A reported plan for "100 GW of solar-powered AI satellites per year" is unverified against a primary source.

### Inferences
- **[ANALYST]** All three reduce AI value to energy throughput, but at different layers:

  | Figure | Layer | Claim |
  |---|---|---|
  | Altman | Compute | Compute is the currency |
  | Huang | Tokens | Tokens per watt is revenue |
  | Musk | Energy | Energy is the true currency |

  Musk's claim is the most fundamental, and the most "physics-first", consistent with his first-principles rhetoric.
- **[ANALYST]** Altman's "intelligence cost converges to electricity cost" is effectively the mechanism behind Musk's chain. If intelligence and robot labor cost about the same as the electricity they use, the price of most goods tends toward their energy (and material) content. That is why energy becomes the meaningful unit.
- **[ANALYST]** Musk's businesses span every layer of this chain: energy generation and storage (Tesla Energy, solar), compute (xAI), robots (Optimus), and space power and compute (SpaceX/Starlink). His thesis is therefore also self-reinforcing as a corporate strategy. Critics make this point (see Q1, TechNewsWorld).

### Gaps
- No primary Huang keynote transcript was obtained. The "tokens per watt" and "performance per watt is revenue" wording comes from secondary reports.
- Whether Musk's 2024 prediction of a 2025 power-generation shortage came true was not assessed.
- No direct Musk reply to Altman's "compute is the currency" remark was found.

## Q4. Musk on the Kardashev scale, solar, and "civilization's progress measured by energy harnessed"

### Takeaway
Musk repeatedly uses the Kardashev scale as his yardstick of civilizational progress. He says humanity harnesses less than 1% of the solar energy reaching Earth. He says that "once you understand the Kardashev Scale" it is "utterly obvious" that essentially all energy will be solar (more than 99% long-term). He frames space-based solar AI as the step toward Type II.

### Cited Findings
- **[MUSK]** X post (Oct 2024): "Solar power is growing super fast. In the very long-term (Kardashev Scale), solar will be >99% of all power generation." — [X @elonmusk 1841122348742680715](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1841122348742680715)
