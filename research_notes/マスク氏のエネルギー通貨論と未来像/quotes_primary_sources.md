## Q1. On which occasions did Musk say money will become irrelevant, unnecessary or "won't matter"?

### Takeaway
The "money becomes irrelevant" line has a clear lineage. It starts with 2020–21 tweets calling money "an information system / database for labor allocation" that AGI would "obviate", and continues through "no job is needed" plus "universal high income" (Sunak, Nov 2023; VivaTech, May 2024; Rogan, Oct 2025). The explicit "money will stop being relevant" line appears at the U.S.-Saudi Investment Forum (19 Nov 2025). It sharpens into "money disappears as a concept" (Kamath, 30 Nov 2025) and finally into a dated claim, "money won't matter in 2036" (The Economist, released ~23 Jul 2026). The most recent version is the "multiplanetary" framing on Walter Isaacson's *The Builders* (released 20 Oct 2026).

## Q2. When and where did Musk say energy, electricity or watts will be the real currency?

### Takeaway
This concept appears from Oct 2025. He first ties it to Bitcoin: "impossible to fake energy" (X, 14 Oct 2025). On the Kamath podcast (30 Nov 2025) it becomes "Energy is the true currency" and "power generation as the de facto currency". It is repeated as an X reply in Dec 2025. On Moonshots (Dec 2025/Jan 2026) it becomes "future currency will essentially just be wattage", with a Kardashev framing. It peaks in the X post of 7 Feb 2026: "Just wattage and tonnage will matter, not dollars." The latest version is the *Builders* line: "currency will be measured in fundamental physics metrics".

## Q3. Statements about physical output, mass to orbit, compute per watt or tokens per watt as units of value

### Takeaway
"Tonnage" and "mass" as value units are documented (Feb 2026 X post; *Builders*, Oct 2026). "Tokens per watt" as a currency is **not** documented. In the Dwarkesh/Cheeky Pint podcast (Feb 2026), Musk framed space as the cheapest place to "generate tokens" and to put AI, which is a cost argument, not a currency argument.

### Cited Findings
- "Just wattage and tonnage will matter, not dollars." (X, 2026-02-07) — [X post](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2020202496547844312)
- Builders (Oct 2026): currency measured in "fundamental physics metrics", i.e. wattage, tonnage, mass and energy. — [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/culture/elon-musk-future-currency-watts-mass-energy)
- Davos (Jan 2026): output equals average productivity per robot times number of robots [P]. — [whatsuptesla transcript](https://whatsuptesla.com/2026/01/27/transcript-elon-musk-at-davos-world-economic-forum-jan-2026/); [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/elon-musk-tells-davos-ai-robots-and-space-will-redefine-the-global-economy/)
- Dwarkesh Podcast x Cheeky Pint (John Collison), released 2026-02-05, "In 36 months, the cheapest place to put AI will be space":
  - A solar panel gives about five times more power in space.
  - Once access to space is cheap, space is "by far the cheapest and most scalable way to generate tokens".
  - Projects a few hundred GW per year of AI in space within about five years.
  - Sources: [Dwarkesh](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/elon-musk); [Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/dwarkesh-podcast-w-elon-musk-on-terawatt-of-gpus-in-space-transcript/); [TechCrunch, 2026-02-05](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/05/elon-musk-is-getting-serious-about-orbital-data-centers/)

### Inferences
- Musk's chain of value runs: solar watts, then robots and chips (tonnage of manufactured matter), then AI compute and tokens, and later space data centres. That is why "watts" and "tons" are his chosen units.

### Gaps
- Searches found no Musk quote using "mass to orbit" or "compute per watt" as a currency or value unit (only as engineering metrics). Flag as unverified if a report claims this.
